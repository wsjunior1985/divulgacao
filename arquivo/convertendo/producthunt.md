## FAQ (pronto para a aba de perguntas do PH)

- **Q:** Do I need an account to convert? — **A:** No, you can convert right away without signing up.
- **Q:** Which payment methods are compared? — **A:** Credit card, prepaid card, debit, cash, remittances (Wise/Nomad style), crypto and PIX — each with the applicable IOF and spread.
- **Q:** What is IOF? — **A:** A Brazilian federal tax on FX operations: 3.38% on international credit card purchases, 1.1% on cash/prepaid, 0.38% on remittances.
- **Q:** Is there a free plan? — **A:** Yes. Free includes currency and crypto conversion and live quotes. Premium adds shopping lists, the barcode/price-tag scanner, unlimited history and no ads.
- **Q:** Is it a PWA? — **A:** Yes, installable and works offline for conversions.
